    How Uber picks who gets the ping

    • ✓Uber waits a few seconds, groups nearby requests and drivers, then matches to cut total pickup time (ETA). It's based on drive time, not straight-line distance.1
    • ≈So park where you can get onto the main road fast. Pick a spot near the garage exit on the lowest covered level. Deep inside a garage or behind a light, you're slower than a driver at the curb.
    • ≈Stay online while you reposition. A pickup behind you costs ETA, one ahead of you doesn't, so drive toward the hotels.

    10-second rule

    Payout $ ≥ total minutes
    • ≈Pickup minutes + trip minutes. 8 + 30 = 38, so it needs $38+. That works out to at least $60/hr for every minute you're on a trip. This is arithmetic, not market data.
    • ≈Pickup over 12 minutes: only if it pays $60+.

    Gas and heat

    • ✓Suburban 5.3L: EPA 14–15 mpg city.6 At $4.95 that's 33–35¢ every mile. 10 empty miles ≈ $3.40.
    • ✓Phoenix-area average regular was $4.70 on Sept 18.7 You're paying $4.89–5.00, which is 20–30¢ over, or $5–7.50 per 25 gallons.
    • ✓Costco gas at 15255 N Hayden Rd is about 1.5 miles from the Kierland garage (membership required).8 Check GasBuddy before you go.
    • ✓In Tempe heat, a car parked 1 hour in shade had about 100°F inside, vs 116°F in sun. Seats were 105°F vs 123°F.9
    • ≈Idling: a 4.6L engine burns 0.39 gal/hr with no load, and AC adds up to 90%.10 For your 5.3L with AC, that's roughly 0.5–0.75 gal/hr, or about $2.50–3.70/hr. Still cheaper than 8 empty miles.
