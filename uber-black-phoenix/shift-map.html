    How Uber picks who gets the ping

    • ✓Uber waits a few seconds, groups nearby requests and drivers, then matches to cut total pickup time (ETA). It's based on drive time, not straight-line distance.1
    • ≈So park where you can get onto the main road fast: near the garage exit, not deep inside.
    • ≈Stay online while you move between spots. You can get matched on the way.
    • ✓Uber needs your GPS to know where you are, and signal drops in tunnels and heavy cover.17 Underground or deep inside a garage, check that the app still shows your dot in the right place.

    10-second rule

    Payout $ ≥ total minutes
    • ≈Pickup minutes + trip minutes. 8 + 30 = 38, so it needs $38+. That's at least $1 a minute, or $60/hr, from accepting to drop-off. If the ride ends far out, add the minutes to drive back too. This is arithmetic, not market data.
    • ✓Apps like GigU, Para and Maxymo grade offers automatically, but Uber says automation tools that access its app aren't allowed, and Lyft has warned drivers using them of deactivation.15 Not worth risking a Black account. The rule above does the same job in your head.

    Gas and heat

    • ✓Suburban with the 5.3L V8 (the usual engine; check yours): EPA 14–15 mpg city.6 At $4.95 that's 33–35¢ every mile. 10 empty miles ≈ $3.40.
    • ✓Phoenix-area average regular was $4.70 on Sept 18.7 You're paying $4.89–5.00, which is 19–30¢ over, or about $4.75–7.50 per 25 gallons.
    • ✓Costco gas at 15255 N Hayden Rd is about 1.5 miles from the Kierland garage by my estimate (membership required).8 Check GasBuddy for its price. Fill up there only when a trip already takes you north.
    • ✓In Tempe heat, a car parked 1 hour in shade had about 100°F inside, vs 116°F in sun. Seats were 105°F vs 123°F.9
    • ≈Idling: a 4.6L engine burns 0.39 gal/hr with no load, and AC adds up to 90%.10 Scaled to a 5.3L with AC, that's roughly 0.45–0.85 gal/hr, or $2.20–4.20/hr at $4.95. An hour of idling costs about the same as 6–12 empty miles.
