10-second rule

Payout $ ≥ total minutes

Add pickup minutes and trip minutes. 8 min + 30 min = 38 min, so it needs $38 or more.

  • Pickup over 12 min: skip unless it pays $60+.
  • Ends far out (Buckeye, Surprise, Queen Creek, San Tan, Maricopa, Anthem): needs $1.25 per minute, since you drive back empty.
  • Never drive more than 8 min to chase surge.
  • Ride ends at PHX: take it, then leave the airport right away for the spot shown above.

Gas in a Suburban

  • At $4.95 a gallon and about 15 mpg in town, every mile costs you about 33¢. 10 empty miles = $3.30.
  • Idling with the AC on burns about $3–4 an hour. Driving around looking for rides burns much more, so park and wait.
  • Wait in a covered garage, not an open lot. It stays 15–20° cooler, so you can go longer with the engine off. When it gets too hot, idle with the AC on recirculate. That's cheaper than driving.
  • When you accept a ride, blast the AC on max recirculate. The drive to the pickup cools the car. If the rider is under 3 minutes away, wait with the engine running.
  • Fill up during the 9:30 lull. Costco and Sam's Club usually run 20–40¢ under the stations near you. Find Costco gas
